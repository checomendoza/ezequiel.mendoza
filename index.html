About

I'm a Senior Software Engineer with more than 15 years of experience building software — from my first web applications in 2010 to complete digital products running in production today.

In 2021 I joined Globant as a Senior Software Developer, working on enterprise applications for global clients within large, distributed teams. In parallel, I design and build products end-to-end: SaaS platforms for gyms and education, mobile apps, multi-tenant systems, payment integrations, admin dashboards and AI-powered features.

My current focus is applied AI — building AI-powered SaaS products with LLMs, embeddings and AI workflows, as well as the AI-driven engineering systems that make AI-assisted development reliable: specification-driven frameworks, multi-agent workflows and document-driven architectures.
